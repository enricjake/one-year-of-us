How this works

A little album that belongs to the two of you.

Add a memory, then tap ✎ Edit on its card to change the date, words, photo, or music. Drag cards to reorder. Everything saves itself to the shared album, so the other device sees your changes within a few seconds. Anyone with the page link can add and edit — treat it like a scrapbook you both hold.
