A

Side A — the beginning

Six months · four tracks

It began with a message on Instagram and a phone call that refused to end. I still have that first screenshot, and I have pretended not to reread it about a hundred times.

You made this face once and I laughed for a full minute. You have done it again every single time since, and it still works. Some tracks never get old.

Purple signs, the hum of the fridge, you wandering the snack aisle like it was a museum. You sent me a photo from the aisle and made me feel like I was standing there with you.

You brought the lightstick and the photocard, and I finally understood why you practise the fanchants in the car. I didn't know a song could make you glow like that.

B

Side B — the rest of it

Six months · four tracks

Green booth, warm light, you stirring that drink with the straw while telling me about your day. I remember the light more than the meal, and that's saying something.

String lights, a gazebo, and you lit up better than the bulbs. You stayed until the staff started stacking chairs and told me about it later. Somehow it felt like we were there together.

Stone pillars, long verandas and nowhere to be. You in a black tee and jeans, looking like you'd lived there all your life. I kept the photo for exactly that reason.

A year ago you were a message on a screen. Now you're the person I turn the camera toward without thinking. Same smile, a whole country between us, no complaints. Let the tape loop.

Liner notes

I made you a tape because a letter felt too short and a playlist felt too easy. Every track on here is a day I'd play again.

Thanks for the snack aisles you sent me, the green booth you told me about, the fanchants in the car, and for making a year feel like the first line of something.

One word for it all: devoted. Happy one year, Nicole. Side C starts now.

— Enric
